CRITICAL READING TEXTS: STUDY SUMMARIES
SIT4200 Principles of Learning and Curriculum Design (Level 7)

These summaries are original study notes written from the PDFs supplied in this workspace. They are for private study. They are not a substitute for reading the books, and they are not copy-and-paste extracts.

Important edition note
The PGCE reading list cites some later editions than the files available here. Each summary states the edition actually read. Do not reference a 2019/2023/2024/2026 edition for page-level claims taken from an earlier PDF.

How to use these notes in the assignment
Read them as a map of each author’s argument. When you write, follow claim → evidence → critique → decision → impact. These books teach the critique step: they show how not to summarise sources one by one (Ridley; Williams), how to treat a claim as conclusion plus warrant (Wallace and Wray), and what Level 7 actually requires (QAA).
